We are more than happy to welcome [Peter Stegmaier](/people/stegmaier/) as a new PhD candidate in the OWL-ML lab. Peter is supervised jointly with [Hochschule Bremen](https://www.hs-bremen.de/), with Prof. Dr.-Ing. Uta Bohnebeck as co-supervisor.

In his PhD, Peter will develop trustworthy AI for marine underway observations: the continuous, high-resolution measurements that German research vessels record along their ship track. Quality control of these data still relies on rule-based checks and manual expert review, which scale poorly as data volumes grow. Peter will build a quality control support system in which anomaly detection points experts to suspicious segments, explainable evidence helps them tell measurement artifacts from real environmental variability, and their feedback continually adapts the model to new cruises and vessels without forgetting what it has learned before. We are looking forward to inspiring future research.
