We are happy to welcome Federico Di Valerio for a PhD internship in the lab. Federico is a PhD candidate at [Sapienza Università di Roma](https://www.uniroma1.it/), Italy, supervised by Prof. Roberto Capobianco, and will be staying at OWL-ML in Bremen until April 2027.

Federico's research lies at the intersection of continual learning and explainable AI. During his PhD visit at OWL-ML, he will work on explainability for continual learning and on multimodal continual learning, in particular how to weigh different modalities when constructing a memory. Since modalities can differ drastically in cost, the guiding question is how much one modality is worth storing over another.
