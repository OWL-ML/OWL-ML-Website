we are extremely excited to be part of the organizer team in the newest effort in advancing the field: the **Continual Causality bridge at AAAI-23: **[https://www.continualcausality.com](https://www.continualcausality.com/)

Continual Causality is a** full two-day event **(**7+8th of February** at AAAI-23 in Washington DC, USA): a "bridge" with the aim of **bringing together** the research fields of **continual learning** and **causality**. Beyond scientific advance, one primary goal is to provide a space for **learning, discussions, and to connect and build a diverse community**. The activities range from traditional tutorials and software labs on the educational side, invited vision talks and posters, to a panel and breakout discussions. All materials will be publicly disseminated as a foundation for the community, and the respectively discussed ideas, challenges and prospects will be aggregated to foster continued exchanges beyond AAAI-23.

**For full information, please visit [https://www.continualcausality.com](https://www.continualcausality.com/)** , where the scientific motivation, bridge format, speakers & labs, program and organizers + advisors are detailed. On the webpage, you can also find a link to **join our Continual Causality Slack**, so that we can already start growing together prior to the first event!

Finally, if you are eager to dive in and participate, there is also a broad **call for participation/papers** available: [https://www.continualcausality.com/](https://www.continualcausality.com/)

**Until November 18th (AOE), we invite 2 page submissions that present positions & visions on linking continual learning & causality**.

For further questions, please feel free to visit the [Continual Causality website](https://www.continualcausality.com/).
