The Open World Lifelong Learning (OWLL) Lab is officially starting in March 2022 as a DEPTH junior research group (Nachwuchsgruppe) within the Hessian Center for Artificial Intelligence ([hessian.AI](https://www.hessian.ai/)) and the state-funded research-cluster The Third Wave of Artificial Intelligence ([3AI](https://www.hessian.ai/projects/3ai-the-third-wave-of-ai/)), both coordinated by Technical University of Darmstadt ([TU Darmstadt](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de)).

OWLL is led by Martin Mundt, who is also a postdoctoral researcher in TU Darmstadt's Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning ([AIML](https://www.aiml.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de)) Lab and will start off with funding for 2 PhD candidates (starting as soon as possible).

The research vision and objectives of OWLL are summarized on the [home page](/).
