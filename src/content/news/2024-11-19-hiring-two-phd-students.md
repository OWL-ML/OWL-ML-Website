We are hiring two fully-funded PhD candidates to build up the new OWL-ML lab at the University of Bremen. The deadline for applications is December 10th, 2024.

The positions are not tied to a specific project and have the flexibility to pursue topics surrounding lifelong machine learning of interest to the group. Overall, these fall into the categories of sustainable, adaptive, robust, and inclusive AI/ML. Example topics are:

- Continual learning
- Knowledge transfer
- Memory
- Dynamic neural architectures
- Learning from heterogeneous data streams
- Neuro-inspired lifelong learning
- Intentional forgetting and unlearning
- Ethical considerations and AI system remediation
- Participatory AI practice
