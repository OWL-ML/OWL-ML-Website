We are very pleased to announce that we are co-organizing the workshop on Continual Lifelong Learning at the Asian Conference on Machine Learning (ACML 2022), this December in hybrid format! The physical conference will be held December 8-10th in Hyderabad, India: [https://www.acml-conf.org/2022/](https://www.acml-conf.org/2022/)

The workshop spans many topics related to continual lifelong learning. There’s a call for papers here (deadline 31 Oct): [https://continual-lifelong-learners.github.io/cfp/](https://continual-lifelong-learners.github.io/cfp/)

We have amazing invited speakers:
* Balaraman Ravindran (Professor of Computer Science, IIT Madras, India)
* Jonghyun Choi (Associate Professor, Yonsei University, South Korea)
* Thang B. (Assistant Professor, Australian National University, Australia)
* Joseph K J (PhD Student, IIT Hyderabad, India

Big thanks to workshop co-organizers: Siddharth Swaroop , Khimya Khetarpal , Peter Nickl , Lu Xu, Emtiyaz Khan.

Through the workshop, we hope to connect Asian researchers in this field together and to the rest of the world. Come join us!
