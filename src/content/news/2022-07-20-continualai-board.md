Group leader Martin Mundt has joined the board of directors of the non-profit [ContinualAI](https://www.continualai.org) organization for the election term of 2022-2024.

Following up on the last years' successes of ContinualAI, the six board members will continue scaling up the organization's efforts to facilitate public access to continual learning, organize events, provide lectures, seminar series, and further push the open-source [Avalanche PyTorch library](https://avalanche.continualai.org).
