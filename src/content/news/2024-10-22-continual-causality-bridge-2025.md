we are happy to co-organize the third edition of the **Continual Causality bridge at AAAI-25**! Continual Causality is a** full two-day event **(**25+26th of February 2025** at AAAI-25 in Philadelphia, USA): a “bridge” with the aim of **bringing together** the research fields of **continual learning** and **causality**. Beyond scientific advance, one primary goal is to provide a space for **learning, discussions, and to connect and build a diverse community**. The activities range from traditional tutorials on the educational side, invited vision talks and contributed ones based on submitted papers, to interactive sessions related to a novel hosted challenge, a speakers panel and community breakout discussions.

**For full information, please visit [https://www.continualcausality.com](https://www.continualcausality.com/)** , where the scientific motivation, bridge format, speakers & tutorials, program, and organizers are detailed. On the webpage, you can also find a link to **join our Continual Causality Slack**, so that we can already start growing together prior to the first event!

Finally, if you are eager to dive in and participate, there is also a broad **call for participation/papers** available: [https://www.continualcausality.com/](https://www.continualcausality.com/)
**Until November 25th, 2024 (AOE), we invite 4 page submissions for both a proceedings and non-archival track**.

For further questions, please feel free to visit the [Continual Causality website](https://www.continualcausality.com/).
