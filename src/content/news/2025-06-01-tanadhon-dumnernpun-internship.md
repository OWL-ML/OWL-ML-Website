We are happy to welcome Tanadhon Dumnernpun to the lab as an undergraduate research summer intern from mid May to end of July. Tanadhon was successful in receiving a competitive scholarship through the Erasmus+ program and follows in the footsteps of many students through the strategic [partnership between University of Bremen and Mahidol University](https://www.uni-bremen.de/en/cooperation/university-cooperation-1/international-partner-universities/strategic-partnerships), Thailand.

During his stay, Tanadhon will explore first algorithmic approaches to tackle the continual confounding problem by means of systematic intervention. The problem itself is a highly challenging scenario, recently outlined in our ICML 2025 spotlight publication "[Where is the Truth? The Risk of Getting Confounded in a Continual World](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06434)", where learner struggle from being exposed to different sets of spurious correlations over time. We are looking forward to the progress that will be made during Tanadhon's internship!
