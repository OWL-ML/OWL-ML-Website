Our paper titled "Return of the normal distribution: Flexible deep continual learning with variational auto-encoders" is now published in Neural Networks, Volume 154, Pages 397-412, October 2022.

In the paper we propose a novel two-stage variational auto-encoder approach to continual learning. Our approach comes with the advantage that previous assumptions in continual learning are optional, but can be accommodated if desired. Respectively, our proposed architecture allows for flexible choices to enable unsupervised, semi-supervised, or fully supervised continual learning. In essence, this fits into our view of continual learning: a framework _should allow_ for flexible inclusion of additional assumptions, while arguably _should not require_ strong simplifications per definition.
