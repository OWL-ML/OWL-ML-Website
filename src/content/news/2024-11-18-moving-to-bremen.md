We are delighted to announce that Martin Mundt has accepted the position of full professor for Lifelong Machine Learning at the [University of Bremen](https://www.uni-bremen.de/).

Consequently, the lab will relocate to form the Open World Lifelong Machine Learning (OWL-ML) group in Bremen from January 2025. The lab’s focus will remain on researching _adaptive_, _sustainable_ & _inclusive_ AI in continuation of its quest towards lifelong machine learning.
