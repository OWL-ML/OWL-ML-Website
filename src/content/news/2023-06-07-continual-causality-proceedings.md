The proceedings, [Proceedings of Machine Learning (PMLR) Volume 208](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v208/), are now publicly available for the AAAI-23 **Continual Causality bridge (**[https://www.continualcausality.com](https://www.continualcausality.com/)).

Proceedings include close to a 100 pages of content that initiates a first bridge between continual learning & causality research. As a special foreword, the organizers have written a [retrospective paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v208/mundt23a/mundt23a.pdf) to summarize the event, trends, and discussions for those who were not able to participate:_ "Continual Causality: A Retrospective of the Inaugural AAAI-23 Bridge Program"; Mundt, Cooper, Dhami, Ribeiro, Smith, Bellot, Hayes. _
