We are beyond excited to share our newest effort in making continual machine learning accessible to the public: Martin Mundt will be presenting a 13 week long course titled “𝐎𝐩𝐞𝐧 𝐖𝐨𝐫𝐥𝐝 𝐋𝐢𝐟𝐞𝐥𝐨𝐧𝐠 𝐋𝐞𝐚𝐫𝐧𝐢𝐧𝐠 - 𝐀 𝐂𝐨𝐧𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐮𝐚𝐥 𝐌𝐚𝐜𝐡𝐢𝐧𝐞 𝐋𝐞𝐚𝐫𝐧𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐂𝐨𝐮𝐫𝐬𝐞", supported by [hessian.AI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hessian-ai-the-hessian-center-for-artificial-intelligence/) & the non-profit [ContinualAI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/continual-ai/) organization.

The general focus of the course will be to provide a comprehensive overview of the breath of factors to consider when moving towards machines that learn continually.
