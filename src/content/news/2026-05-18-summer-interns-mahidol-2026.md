We are happy to welcome Zwe Nyan Zaw and Pongsakorn Arayasak to the lab as undergraduate research summer interns from mid May to end of July. Zwe and Pongsakorn were both successful in a competitive application to follow in the footsteps of many students through the strategic [partnership between University of Bremen and Mahidol University](https://www.uni-bremen.de/en/cooperation/university-cooperation-1/international-partner-universities/strategic-partnerships), Thailand.

During the stay at the OWL-ML lab, they will jointly be working on enhancing scientific question-answering and effectively deploying large language models to extract information about continual learning approaches from public papers.
